August 2026

Thursday, August 6th

On this first day, I simply took a placement test through the Goethe-Institut (linked if you wish to try) to find out my current approximate German level. I mainly used context clues and filled in some blanks that I didn't know, so I think the score given might be somewhat presumptuous due to luck.

To see what I have learned up to this point, see the My Journey page.

In this upcoming month, I hope to learn about the accusative case, properly differentiating between kein and nicht, learn more vocabulary, and possibly touch up on different words' plural forms.

Monthly Progress

September 2026

Saturday, September 5th
"Schritt für Schritt" (Step by Step)

In this past month, I with a lot of things regarding German. With school starting, I also found it hard to stay consistent with Anki and Nicos Weg, but I still managed to complete them every other day at the very least, with weekends as rest days. I managed to advance my vocabulary and grammar knowledge, practicing writing along the way as well. My biggest step was learning the basics of the accusative case, which I had been struggling with for a while. Although this knowledge is limited to simple situations, I am still proud of myself for learning it and will continue to practice and improve my understanding of it in this upcoming month. More things I have learned are listed on the Grammar page and on the Vocabulary page.

In the upcoming month, I hope to learn more about the accusative case, learn more vocabulary, and continue to practice writing in German. I also hope to start learning about the dative case, which has been a struggle for me in the past. More things I wish to learn are listed on the Grammar page.

Monthly Progress

October 2026

Sunday, October 4th
"Konstanz ist entscheidend" (Consistency is key)

In this last month, I was really consistent with my German learning. I created an effective study routine for balancing German learning, schoolwork, and my other extracurriculars, and have so far stuck to it consistently. I used Nicos Weg and Anki quite a lot, which I think is great. However, I wasn't as consistent with my listening practice on Easy German as I would have liked. I think my biggest step this past month was learning the basics of the dative case, as it is a fundamental part of German grammar that affects word conjugations. I am proud of the progress I have made so far, and I look forward to continuing my German learning journey in the upcoming months. More things I have learned are listed on the Grammar page and on the Vocabulary page.

In the upcoming month, I hope to learn more about using the accusative and dative cases in more complex situations, utilize the future tense correctly, and practice more on the other tenses as well (such as perfect present and past tenses). More things I wish to learn are listed on the Grammar page.
