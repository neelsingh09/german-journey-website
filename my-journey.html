My Journey

Everyone starts somewhere. This is a record of where I started, where I am now, and where I hope to go with German. Scroll to the bottom to see my end goal and future plans with this website.

The Beginning

Why German?

I have always been fascinated by Germany. Something about the rigidness, structure, and rules of the language draws me in. I used to dream about living there one day, although I'm not sure if that is still the case.

Regardless, I want to stay true to my old self and learn German either way.

Where I Started

What I Already Know

Prior to the summer of 2026, I took a German 1 class in 9th grade in person and a German 2 class in 10th grade online. However, I know that online classes don't allow me to retain information as well as in-person classes, so much of what I learned is basically nonexistent in my memory.

Even so, I have some basic German communication skills, including numbers, introductions, basic descriptions, and basic verb conjugation.

Summer 2026

Starting Again

Here, I begin my effort in learning German. I am currently around a high A1 level, or possibly a low A2 level. This is the time that I started to fully use all of my Resources. This website is part of my new effort to take learning German more seriously and keep track of my progress along the way.

The Goal

Reaching B1

My first and foremost goal is to reach B1-level proficiency in German by the time my first year of college starts. I want this to be verified and certified through an official CEFR exam.

By the end of the summer of 2027, I want to pass an A2-level CEFR exam as a milestone toward reaching B1.

By the end of the summer of 2028, I want to pass a B1-level CEFR exam, which will serve as the finale of this website. After passing, I will attempt to translate this website entirely into German.

Future

What's Next?

There is still a lot for me to learn. As I continue studying German, I'll use this website to document the milestones, challenges, and changes in my goals along the way.
