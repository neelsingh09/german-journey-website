Grammar

Grammar is, to me, one of the more difficult parts of learning German. There are different cases, verb forms, tenses, sentence structures, and a lot of other rules to keep track of.

Here's a look at some of the grammar I have learned recently and what I hope to learn next.

What I've Recently Learned

Note: This is not a list of every grammar rule I have ever learned. I am restricting this list to a max of 5 items, and any items past those 5 will be booted.

  • The dative case (in simple scenarios)
  • Stating directions
  • Discussing times/dates
  • Talking about work
  • Describing appointments

What I Hope to Learn Next

  • The dative case (in more complex scenarios)
  • The accusative case (in more complex scenarios)
  • Leisure
  • Talking about physical orientation
  • Future tense
