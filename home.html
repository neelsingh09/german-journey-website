Welcome to German Learning

Hello! I am Neel Singh, and this is my website documenting my progress in learning German. Currently, my CEFR level is around high A1.

My goal for this website is to regularly update the Progress page with monthly updates on how my German learning has been going. Ideally, I will make these updates during the first week of each month.

Each update will include a picture related to something I talk about, along with a paragraph or two about what I have learned, what I am currently learning, and potentially what I plan to learn in the upcoming month.

I will also update the Vocabulary page with new words that I have learned, and the Resources page with any new resources that I have found useful in my learning.

The Grammar page will also be updated with new grammar concepts that I have learned and hope to learn in the future.
