Grammar

Grammar is, to me, one of the more difficult parts of learning German. There are different cases, verb forms, tenses, sentence structures, and a lot of other rules to keep track of.

Here's a look at some of the grammar I have learned recently and what I hope to learn next.

What I've Learned

Note: This is not a list of every grammar rule I have ever learned. I am restricting this list to a max of 5 items, and any items past those 5 will be removed.

  • The conjugation of regular verbs
  • Usage of der, die, and das
  • The nominative case
  • Placement of different parts of speech

What I Hope to Learn Next

  • The dative case
  • Usage of dem, der, and den
  • The accusative case
  • Correct usage of plurals and commas
